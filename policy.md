# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

## 隐私政策

2026-09-16.1

本政策按应用当前的实际功能说明数据处理。应用没有自建用户账号、广告投放或营销订阅系统，也不以出售个人数据为业务。

### 1. 本地文件与应用数据

应用读取你选择的音乐目录及文件，以建立音乐库、播放音频和显示元数据。文件路径、标签、播放列表、播放记录与统计、输出设备偏好、音效设置、歌词和封面缓存保存在本机。

标签编辑、转换或保存内容时，应用会按相应操作写入目标位置。本政策不将这些本地操作描述为向开发者上传文件。

本地音频文件、音乐库数据库及播放记录不自动上传；在线匹配仅发送必要的曲目搜索信息。

### 2. 网络连接与商店

启用联网功能、执行在线操作或打开外部链接时，会产生完成相应功能所需的网络请求；接收请求的服务可能获得 IP 地址等连接信息。你可以在设置中关闭自动联网相关功能，或不使用相应在线操作；本地播放不要求向开发者注册账号。

Microsoft Store 负责商店账号、支付及订单处理。应用读取商店提供的产品许可、试用到期信息和购买结果，用于展示授权状态和控制付费功能；开发者不通过本应用接收你的银行卡资料或 Microsoft 账号密码。平台的数据处理适用其隐私政策。

### 3. 日志与主动反馈

应用在本机记录运行和错误日志，可能包含文件路径、曲目信息、设备名称、错误堆栈等排查资料。当前日志配置按日滚动，最多保留 30 个日志文件。

应用不自动向开发者上传这些日志。只有你主动通过邮箱等渠道提供日志、截图或问题描述时，开发者才会收到相关内容。发送前请检查并遮盖不希望提供的信息。反馈资料用于排查和回复请求，不用于营销；你可联系开发者请求访问、更正或删除，法律要求保留的情况除外。

### 4. 保存位置与管理

主要数据库、设置及日志位于用户文档目录下的 OriginalSoundPlayer 文件夹；缓存位置可在应用设置中查看或更改。协议同意记录位于应用可访问的本地应用数据目录下 OriginalSoundPlayer/agreement.json，包含版本、语言和 UTC 同意时间。

你可以在应用中管理音乐库与缓存，或退出应用后备份、删除相应应用数据。删除数据库或设置会丢失本地列表、统计或偏好；删除协议记录会在下次启动时重新要求确认。卸载不保证自动删除文档目录和自定义缓存中的文件。

### 5. 官网与外部链接

官网通过 GitHub Pages 提供，访问时托管平台会处理提供网站所需的连接信息。本官网代码未接入广告追踪或访问分析脚本。点击商店、GitHub 或其他外链后，相应服务按其自身规则处理访问。

我们采取与应用规模和处理方式相适应的措施保护数据，但不能承诺任何设备、存储或网络传输绝对安全。请保护自己的 Windows 账号及备份。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用及购买。

### 6. 更新与联系

数据处理方式发生重大变化时，会更新本政策并提供适当通知；需要单独同意的用途，不以接受一般用户协议代替。关于隐私或主动提供的反馈资料，请联系 dannypan9709@foxmail.com。

## Privacy Policy

2026-09-16.1

This policy describes data handling for the app's current features. The app has no developer-operated user accounts, advertising or marketing subscription system, and selling personal data is not its business.

### 1. Local files and app data

The app reads music folders and files you select to build your library, play audio and display metadata. File paths, tags, playlists, playback history and statistics, device preferences, effects settings, lyrics and artwork caches are stored locally.

Editing tags, converting or saving content writes to the destination for that operation. This policy does not describe those local operations as uploading files to the developer.

Local audio files, the library database and playback history are not automatically uploaded. Online matching sends only the track search information needed for that operation.

### 2. Network connections and the Store

Enabling online features, performing online actions or opening external links makes the network requests needed for those functions. The receiving service may obtain connection information such as your IP address. You can disable automatic online features in Settings or avoid the relevant online actions. Local playback does not require registering an account with the developer.

Microsoft Store handles Store accounts, payments and orders. The app reads the product license, trial expiration and purchase results supplied by the Store to display licensing status and control paid features. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Microsoft account password through this app. The platform's own privacy policy governs its processing.

### 3. Logs and voluntary support requests

Runtime and error logs are written locally and may include file paths, track information, device names, exception details and other diagnostic information. The current configuration rolls logs daily and retains up to 30 log files.

The app does not automatically upload these logs to the developer. The developer receives logs, screenshots or descriptions only when you choose to send them, for example by email. Review and redact them before sending. Support information is used to investigate and reply, not for marketing. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to legally required retention.

### 4. Storage and controls

The main database, settings and logs are under OriginalSoundPlayer in your Documents folder. The cache location can be viewed or changed in Settings. The acceptance record is stored in OriginalSoundPlayer/agreement.json under the local application data directory available to the app; it contains the version, language and UTC acceptance time.

Manage your library and caches in the app, or exit before backing up or deleting app data. Removing databases or settings loses local lists, statistics or preferences. Removing the acceptance record prompts for acceptance again at the next launch. Uninstalling does not guarantee removal of files in Documents or custom cache folders.

### 5. Website and external links

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator processes connection information to serve the site. This website's code does not integrate advertising trackers or traffic analytics scripts. When you follow Store, GitHub or other external links, the destination processes the visit under its own policies.

We take measures appropriate to the app and its processing, but no device, storage or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your Windows account and backups. Minors should use and purchase the app with their guardian's guidance.

### 6. Changes and contact

Material changes to data handling will be reflected here with appropriate notice. Uses requiring separate consent will not rely on acceptance of general terms. For privacy questions or information you have voluntarily supplied, contact dannypan9709@foxmail.com.
